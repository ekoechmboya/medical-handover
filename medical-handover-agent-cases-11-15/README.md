# Medical Handover Quality Benchmark — Cases 11–15

Five hard synthetic benchmark cases designed to test whether an AI handover-quality agent can:

- distinguish resolved abnormalities from active risks
- reason across transfers between departments
- identify medication discontinuation and safety restrictions
- rank multiple pending items by clinical urgency
- reason over conflicting timelines and noisy historical information
- avoid false positives caused by stale or misleading records

All data is fictional and synthetic and is intended for engineering evaluation only.
